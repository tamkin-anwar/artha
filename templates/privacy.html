Artha

Privacy & Security

What actually happens to your data when you use Artha, in plain language, not legal boilerplate.

Siloed by account

Your transactions, accounts and balances, notes, and scenarios are scoped to your account alone. No other user's queries can ever touch them.

Encrypted in transit

Every page loads over HTTPS, and session cookies are marked Secure, so they never travel over plain HTTP.

No tracking, no ads

Zero analytics or advertising scripts run anywhere on Artha. Nothing about how you use it gets sold or shared.

AI doesn't train on your data

Anthropic's API, which powers Artha's AI features, does not use customer data to train its models by default. Artha never opts in.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter into Artha, transactions, account balances, notes, scenarios, calendar events, lives in a single Postgres database hosted on Render, a SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified infrastructure provider. It isn't shared with any other product, and nobody outside of running the app has a reason to open it.

How your account is protected

  • ✓Your password is hashed, never stored in plain text. Not even Artha's own operator can read it back.
  • ✓Two-factor authentication is optional and free: a code from an authenticator app on top of your password, with one-time recovery codes for a lost phone.
  • ✓Login is rate-limited, so repeated password guesses get slowed down automatically.
  • ✓Deleting your account is real: a 30-day window, undone just by logging back in, then a full purge, not a flag that quietly leaves your data behind.

Your bank statement, step by step

  1. 1.You upload a PDF or CSV. It's read directly from the upload into memory, never written to disk or any storage bucket, anywhere.
  2. 2.A free, offline parser reads it first, entirely on Artha's own server. No external call, no cost, for the large majority of statements.
  3. 3.Only if that fails, the extracted text, never the original file, is sent once to Claude to make sense of an unusual layout.
  4. 4.You review every row before anything is saved. Edit or uncheck any line first.
  5. 5.You click Import, or you don't. Close the tab instead, and nothing from that statement was ever saved.

The AI Assistant

When you ask it something, it's handed a snapshot built for that one question, your budgets, upcoming notes and events, and recent transactions, not a permanent memory of everything you've ever entered. It runs on Anthropic's commercial API, which by policy does not use customer data to train its models unless a customer explicitly opts in. Artha doesn't.

More importantly: the AI Assistant can never write to your account directly. Every action it suggests, adding a transaction, setting a budget, anything, shows up as a card you have to click to confirm. It proposes; you decide.

The iPhone app

  • ✓Your password isn't kept on the phone. Signing in gives that one iPhone its own key, held in the iPhone's Keychain. Artha's server stores only a one-way hash of it, and signing out, changing your password, or deleting your account turns it off.
  • ✓A copy of your latest numbers stays on the phone so the app opens instantly. It's encrypted whenever the phone is locked, and erased when you sign out. The copy your widgets read is unlocked after the first unlock since the phone last restarted, so Lock Screen widgets can show it.
  • ✓Widgets show only what you put there. A Lock Screen widget shows its number while the phone is locked, so only add one if you're comfortable with that.
  • ✓Apple Pay logging is off until you set it up in the Shortcuts app yourself. When it's on, Artha receives only what Wallet shares with the automation: the amount, its currency, and the store's name. Never your card number, and never a bank login.
  • ✓You can create and delete your account right in the app. Signing up asks for the same things the website does. Deleting from the app works the same way too: it asks for your password, signs out every device, and starts the same 30-day window.
  • ✓Notifications are made on your iPhone. Bill reminders, budget alerts and Apple Pay confirmations are created by the app from numbers it already has, so no notification service ever sees your money. Now and then iOS lets the app refresh in the background to keep them current.
  • ✓No analytics or tracking in the app either. It talks only to Artha's own server.

What Artha doesn't do

  • ✕Doesn't sell or share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else.
  • ✕Doesn't run analytics or ad-tracking scripts of any kind.
  • ✕Doesn't ask for your bank login, on the web or in the app. There's no bank connection to give one to.
  • ✕Doesn't store your bank statement file anywhere, ever, not even temporarily.
  • ✕Doesn't let the AI touch your account without your own explicit click.
  • ✕Doesn't let one user's data cross into another's, even on the same Artha instance.
